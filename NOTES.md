## FRONTEND ARCHITECTURE

### Pages
In the /src/pages folder, we will categories pages according to following rules:
1. Global pages (not part of a product) will be in their own folders in the /src/pages folder.

2. Product Specific pages (part of a product) will be in their own folders inside their product folder like /src/pages/VM/pages/Vulns.

3. Lazy Load pages when added them to routes.

3. If any team member wants to change color on any component, he should use the appropriate colors/create appropriate colors in the reui.css file (/src/css/config.reui.css) file. Also make sure it has both light & dark versions.

4. If any one has to install an NPM package, he has to take permission from "shoaib"
